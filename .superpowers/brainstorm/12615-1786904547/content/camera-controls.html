How much freedom should the city camera have?

All three keep an orthographic isometric view as the starting point. The difference is whether the reader can orbit away from the civic-map context.

A · Locked civic map

Pan + zoom only

Most legible. Parcels always read as a map. Lowest interaction risk, but less like an explorable city.

Recommended

B · Constrained orbit

Isometric default + shallow orbit

Best balance. Preserve the civic map at rest; allow a bounded orbit for depth, inspection, and the Draxul-like feel.

C · Full explorer

Orbit + perspective toggle

Most cinematic. Closest to a game-like city, but it can make boundaries and impact routes harder to compare.
